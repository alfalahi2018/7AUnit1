FEYNMAN CHALLENGE

Present Continuous for Future Arrangements

Understand it • Explain it • Use it

🎯 Challenge goals

Explain and use the Present Continuous to talk about a fixed future arrangement, often with a time or place.

⏱ Time: 15–20 minutes
👥 Grouping: Pairs or small groups

1. Understand the rule

🧩 FORM

Subject + am / is / are + verb-ing

I am meeting my cousin tomorrow.
She is visiting the dentist on Monday.

📅 WHEN?

Use it for a future plan that is already arranged. It often includes a time or place.

We are playing football after school.

Time expressions: tonight, tomorrow, this weekend, on Friday, at 5 p.m.

2. Explain it to a younger student

Complete the ideas in your own words.

3. Choose the correct answer

Choose the best answer and check your score.

1. I ___ my friend at the mall tomorrow.
2. They ___ their grandparents on Friday.
3. ___ she ___ the bus at 7 a.m. tomorrow?
4. We ___ football tonight; the match is arranged.

4. Fix the mistake

Correct these sentences. You can write your answers below.

a) I meeting my teacher tomorrow.

b) She are going to the library on Saturday.

c) Are they visit us tonight?

Suggested answers:
a) I am meeting my teacher tomorrow.
b) She is going to the library on Saturday.
c) Are they visiting us tonight?

5. Final mission: You are the teacher! 🎤

Explain the rule to a partner. Then tell them three arrangements you have this week. Include a day or time in each sentence.

  1. I am ____________________________________.
  2. I am ____________________________________.
  3. I am ____________________________________.

Bonus: Ask a partner: “What are you doing this weekend?” Then report their answer.
